Nobody says it out loud. But they feel it.

If your website looks like a template, people assume you’re just another template too. They’ve judged your whole business before they’ve read a single word.

A cookie-cutter site is like a stock photo smile: technically fine, but instantly forgettable.

That would be okay—if your website wasn’t supposed to represent you. But it does. And people scroll on.

Your clients aren’t looking for perfection. They’re looking for something they can believe in. Something that feels human. Unique. Real.

A template site isn’t just a design problem. It’s a missed opportunity to stand out. And standing out is how trust begins.
